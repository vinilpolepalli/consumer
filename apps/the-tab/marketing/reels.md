# The Tab — three ready-to-film reels

All three filmable by one person with one phone in under an hour total.
Shared prep (10 min, do once):

- Charge phone, Do Not Disturb ON, dark room, one warm lamp. Face shots are lit by
  the phone screen itself — no ring light.
- Open the live app URL, run one throwaway tab, then start a New Tab so inputs are
  clean for camera. "Run it again" replays the reveal from stored inputs — retakes
  are free, use them.
- Screen recordings: iOS Control Center recorder, microphone OFF. The reveal was
  built silence-proof — the odometer roll and flip carry without music. Do not add a
  trending sound over the roll; add it (quiet) only over the face beats if at all.
- Know this before filming: on iPhone the flip's vibration does not fire (iOS ignores
  `navigator.vibrate`) — the visual thud is the beat, and it reads on camera. Never
  say "feel the haptic" in a script.
- Rule for all captions: link in bio and pinned comment, never watermarked on the
  video. Every caption admits the number is an estimate — the receipt already does.

## Reel 1 — "$848,137" (format: The gut-punch stat reveal)

**This is the flagship. Film and post this one first.**

**Hook line (0.0–1.5s, face-cam with on-screen text, sound-off legible):**

> I'm 24 and my DoorDash habit is costing me $848,137.

**Shot-by-shot:**

| Time | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5s | Face-cam, deadpan, hook line as bold on-screen text. | Say it flat. The number does the acting. |
| 1.5–4.5s | Screen recording: type "DoorDash", tap the $19 preset chip, tap **4× a week**, drag age slider to 24. Footer live-updates "≈ $3,952 a year · 41 yrs on the tab". | One continuous take, real speed, no cuts. Leave the footer in frame — it pre-answers "how is this calculated". |
| 4.5–7s | Tap **RUN MY TAB**. Print stub feeds in, odometer columns slam left-to-right into LIFETIME TAB **$162,032**. Hold the hard stop. | Do not cut during the roll. The half-second freeze after the last column lands is the reel. |
| 7–9s | The flip: visual thud to IF YOU'D INVESTED IT **$848,137** BY AGE 65, then the CVS-length receipt unrolls past the bottom of the frame. | Let it unroll off-screen. Length is the joke. |
| 9–13s | Hard cut to face-cam, silent, hands on head. Small on-screen text: "that's a house. in doordash." | That line is printed on the receipt — the app wrote your reaction for you. This is the duet/stitch frame. |
| 13–15s | End on the 9:16 share receipt full-frame. Text: "run your own tab. comment a habit — I'll run it next." | The card is the outro. No logo animation. |

**Caption:**

> I'm not saying stop ordering. I'm saying I finally looked. Free, in bio, and the
> receipt labels the math itself — 7% long-run average, an estimate, not advice.
> Comment a habit and a price. I'll run your tab next.

**Why this format fits:** The app is this reel's machine — `RUN MY TAB` ships as a
verbatim string, the odometer/flip/unroll choreography exists for exactly this
15 seconds, and "Run it again" exists so you can retake the money shot. Every viewer
has a different habit and a different number, so the comments become a queue of
free follow-up content.

## Reel 2 — "Roasted by a receipt" (format: Brutal honesty)

**Hook line (0.0–1.5s, on-screen text over the receipt's roast line, zoomed):**

> I just got roasted by a receipt.

**Shot-by-shot:**

| Time | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5s | Extreme close-up of a printed roast line on the receipt, hook text above it. | Shoot the screen straight on, paper fills the frame. |
| 1.5–4s | Screen recording: type "vapes", $15, **Every day**, run it. Roll → flip. | Fast. This reel's star is the copy, not the choreography — Reel 1 owns the slow reveal. |
| 4–7s | Slow zoom down the receipt to the roast line. Cut to face: one eyebrow. Nothing said. | The app roasts the habit, never the person — your reaction sells that it still lands. |
| 7–11s | "And it has limits." Screen recording: type "bottle service", $2,400. The app answers on-screen: "That's not a habit, that's a payroll. Running it anyway." Run it — the number is obscene. | The easter egg is shipped behavior. Absurd inputs play along on camera by design — no error states, ever. |
| 11–15s | Face-cam: "Comment your habit and the price. I'll run it and read you your roast." End frozen on the receipt barcode + THANK YOU FOR YOUR HABITS. | The footer line is the punchline — let viewers find it. |

**Caption:**

> It roasts the habit. Never you. That's the one rule. Comment a habit + what one
> round costs — I'll reply with your roast line. Free in bio. Every number on the
> receipt is labeled an estimate.

**Why this format fits:** Brutal honesty is usually a moderation risk — here it's a
shipped, bounded feature: a roast line prints on every receipt, essentials are never
roasted, and the $2,000 easter egg means hostile-input stunts make the app look
better, not broken. "I'll read you your roast" turns the comment section into an
endless reply-reel factory with zero new ideas needed.

## Reel 3 — "Tab Duel" (format: Tag-a-friend result)

**Hook line (0.0–1.5s, the VS receipt full-frame, text on top):**

> We put both our habits on one receipt. One of us owes an apology.

**Shot-by-shot:**

| Time | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Prep | Run Tab Duel once off-camera so you know your verdict. Save the 9:16 duel card. | 3 minutes. No second person needed on camera — you run both habits yourself. |
| 0–1.5s | The finished VS receipt, both columns visible, hook text over it. | The two-column layout is the scroll-stopper — it reads as a fight card. |
| 1.5–4.5s | Screen recording: run habit one fast — "her matcha", $8, 5× a week, age. Roll lands. | Jump-cut through input. Speed here makes the duel feel like a game. |
| 4.5–7.5s | Tap **Tab duel**, run habit two — "my DoorDash", $19, 4× a week. | Same pace. |
| 7.5–11s | The VS receipt assembles: both columns, THE GAP amount, the invested-gap hero, the verdict line. Slow zoom on the verdict. | Hold the verdict a full beat. It names a winner — that's the argument-starter. |
| 11–15s | Face-cam, deadpan: "Loser invests the difference." Text: "tag who you're dueling." End on the 9:16 duel card. | Couples, roommates, coworkers — the tag does the distribution. |

**Caption:**

> Tag your duel. Don't explain it, just tag them. Loser invests the gap. Free, in
> bio — both numbers say estimate right on the receipt, because they are.

**Why this format fits:** Duel mode was built free, in v1, specifically as the
two-person artifact — VS columns, gap amount, verdict line, shareable in both
aspects. Tag-a-friend outsources reach to the comment section, and a duel receipt is
a fight both people repost: the winner gloats, the loser demands a rematch with a
different habit. Post after Reel 1's comments show pairs tagging each other.

## Formats deliberately skipped

- **POV demo:** no camera/pointing mechanic — the screen-recorded reveal already is
  the demo, and Reel 1 spends it better.
- **Founder build-in-public:** held in reserve as the day-3+ series ("I built an app
  that itemizes your regrets — day 4, someone ran their tab at 2 AM"), once Reels 1–2
  give the account a spine. It needs no script written today.
